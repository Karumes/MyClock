Hi, I am Katsu, the designer and developer behind Karumes Clock.

I created this platform to bring highly customizable, atmospheric, and visually stunning clocks to your desktop. As a solo developer, I built every clock from scratch with smooth mathematical rendering, responsive layouts, and high-frame-rate rendering techniques.

    • Introduced a dynamic settings menu to customize colors, surfaces, and fonts.
    • Provided a native Windows screensaver setup script for effortless installation.
